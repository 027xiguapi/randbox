### Q2: Why sometimes get same result multiple times in a row?
**A**: This is a normal mathematical phenomenon, **birthday paradox** explains this well.

Imagine a room with 23 people, the probability of two people sharing the same birthday is over 50%. This seems counter-intuitive but is mathematically correct. Similarly, although the probability of generating the same random number consecutively is low, it will inevitably appear in large numbers of trials.

**Statistical Perspective**:
- Probability of getting same result twice consecutively: 1/range value
- Probability of at least one consecutive pair in 1000 trials: about 63%
- This is called "Poisson distribution" rare events

**Actual Suggestions**:
- High-quality random number generators ensure long-term uniform distribution
- "Consecutive numbers" in short term is normal phenomenon
- If concerned, can increase random number range
